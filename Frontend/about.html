  • • Independent Cluster Matrices: Each cluster maintains its own customizable dynamic benchmarking columns and paper queues.

SECTION IV

PRISMA 2020 Protocol & Blind Dual-Review Consensus

Systematic Literature Reviews (SLRs) require rigorous compliance with the international PRISMA 2020 methodology. LitNexis automates multi-stage screening, inclusion/exclusion criteria logging, and inter-rater reliability calculations.

  • • Multi-Stage Screening Funnel: Real-time tracking through Identification, Screening, Eligibility, and Final Included stages.
  • • Blind Independent Screening: Multiple researchers evaluate papers independently without seeing peer decisions until unblinding.
  • • Cohen's Kappa ($\kappa$) Metric: Live computation of statistical inter-rater agreement for publication-ready methodology sections.

SECTION VI

Role-Based Research Collaboration (RBAC)

Collaborate seamlessly across research laboratories and advisory committees with four specialized academic sub-roles designed to preserve editorial integrity and prevent accidental data overwrites.

  • • Owner (Lead Author): Full administrative control, team invitations, column definitions, and survey deletion.
  • • Editor (Co-Author): Can edit matrix values, add new papers, and attach PDF source highlights.
  • • Reviewer (Advisor / Supervisor): Can submit blind PRISMA screening votes and add peer comments without altering raw cells.
  • • Viewer (Public Guest): Read-only access for replication packages and paper appendices.
